Questions

Before you ask.

Can I see it before I buy?

Yes. Book a demo and we will walk you through it on sample data, and answer every question for your agency.

Is my team's information safe?

Every agency's records are kept apart from every other agency's, and each person only sees what their role allows. Your records sit in a database hosted in Mumbai.

Will it work on my team's phones?

Yes. It runs in the phone's browser and can be added to the home screen, where it opens full screen like any other app. There is nothing to download from an app store.

How many people can I add?

Up to 200 on every plan. People who have left do not count against that number.

What if I stop paying?

We pause your workspace. We do not delete it. Pay again and everything comes back as it was.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. You can move up from the Billing screen inside the app whenever you are ready for more.

What do I need to start?

Just an email address and your agency's name. Everything else, from your team to your clients, you add inside the app.

Who builds this?

Nevorai, an Indian software company that builds the software that runs businesses. Nevorai CRM for Agencies is built from the system a real working agency uses every day.
